digiemu 0.4.0 runs two more Elektron instruments, the **Model:Cycles** and the **Model:Samples**, and is the first release with a **macOS app**, for Apple silicon, signed and notarized by Apple. It also fixes **crackle on the Digitone**.

## Get started

**Windows**

1. Download **digiemu-win64-0.4.0.zip** below and unzip it anywhere you can write to, except a OneDrive folder.
2. Get the firmware from Elektron's website:
   - `Digitakt_OS1.53.syx` for the Digitakt;
   - `Digitone_and_Digitone_Keys_OS1.44.syx` (or 1.43) for the Digitone;
   - `model-cycles_OS1.13.syx` for the Model:Cycles;
   - `model-samples_OS1.13.syx` for the Model:Samples.
3. Run `digiemu.exe`, click **Add firmware** and pick the `.syx`. Setup runs once per firmware. On a desktop it takes about 25 seconds for a Digitakt or Digitone, 15 for a Model:Samples and 35 for a Model:Cycles.
4. Click **Play**.

The exe is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen asks first: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*. A PC with Smart App Control turned on blocks it. Tested on Windows 11, 64-bit.

**macOS (Apple silicon)**

1. Download **digiemu-macos-arm64-0.4.0.dmg** below, open it, and drag **digiemu** to **Applications**. It needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
2. Get the firmware from Elektron's website, as above.
3. Open digiemu, click **Add firmware** and pick the `.syx`.
4. Click **Play**.

The app is signed and notarized, so macOS asks only once, as it does for any app from the internet. It keeps your firmware folders, sessions and logs in `~/Library/Application Support/digiemu`.

**Upgrading from 0.3.0 on Windows:** unzip 0.4.0 into a new folder, then move the `firmware` folder from your 0.3.0 folder into it. A firmware set up with 0.3.0 opens where you left off, without being set up again.

## What's new

- **Model:Cycles and Model:Samples, OS 1.13.**
  - Both boot to their own screen and play their patterns, with live 48 kHz audio and MIDI in and out, as on the Digitakt.
  - They share one window:
    - VOLUME (the instrument's own knob) and LEVEL/DATA;
    - the fourteen parameter knobs, the keys and the transport;
    - the six pads (**where you click on a pad sets how hard it is hit**);
    - the sixteen trig keys and the key LEDs.
  - On the Model:Samples, LOAD SAMPLES puts WAV files in `/incoming`. In the sample browser, WAVE opens it, LEVEL/DATA moves through it, and the key under PITCH (its push switch) opens a folder or picks a sample.
  - The Model:Samples' factory samples are not part of its firmware, so its factory patterns are silent until you load samples of your own. The Model:Cycles' factory sounds are in its firmware and play straight away.
  - **Check firmware...** works on both.
- **The macOS app.** It does everything the Windows app does, for Apple silicon Macs, and is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. MIDI also offers a virtual port named after the instrument, for a DAW.
- **No more crackle on the Digitone.** Under live audio the main processor could read the second processor's voices while it was still rendering them, which was heard as crackle.
  - The main processor now waits for the render, and each render starts as early as it can, so the Digitone still plays at full speed.
  - Measured here: a 6.5-second recording of the factory pattern had 25 to 29 glitches at the same point in every 32-sample block, and now has none.
  - Thanks to @amorgan101010, who found and fixed it.

## Known limits

- **The Models:**
  - USB audio, USB MIDI, the battery (Power Handle), and a pad's pressure after it is hit are not modelled.
  - Their keys carry the names the firmware's factory test gives them, which may differ from what is printed on the panel.
- **MIDI:**
  - a buffer the firmware sends by DMA goes out at once, not at 31250 baud;
  - there is no USB MIDI;
  - OUT PORT FUNC's DIN 24 and DIN 48 sync modes are not modelled;
  - on Windows, MIDI data passing through a device has not been tested yet.
- **Tested releases** are Digitakt OS 1.53, Digitone OS 1.43 and 1.44, and Model:Cycles and Model:Samples OS 1.13. Digitakt OS 1.54 sets up and boots once you confirm, but has not been tested further. Other releases of these products also run once you confirm; other Elektron products are recognised and turned away.
- The Digitone runs as a plain Digitone. The Digitone Keys' keyboard, wheels and extra keys are not there.
- The Digitakt's factory *sample* library lives on the real device's storage, not in the firmware, so `/factory` is empty, as on the Model:Samples.
- A 48 kHz sample plays at the right pitch. Whether 44.1 kHz samples do is not verified yet.
- The macOS app passes its self-test, but has not yet been tried with a firmware on a real Mac. There is no build for Intel Macs.
- Control Flow Guard is cleared in both Windows exes, because Unicorn's `longjmp` fails under it. The process then has the same protections as `python.exe`.
